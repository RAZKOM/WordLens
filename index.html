Quick tips

        
    
          
  1. 
            Swipe, then tap
            Move along the keys; brackets mark the one you are on. A tap types it, or ENTER guesses.
          
    2. 
          
  2. 
            Hold
            Deletes the last letter, without scrolling back to DEL.
          
    3. 
          
  3. 
            Tap, then hold
            Opens the menu. Enter guess and Backspace sit at the top, so either is one tap away.
          
    4. 
          
  4. 
            Double-tap
            Asks to leave. Confirm on the glasses and the phone page closes too.
