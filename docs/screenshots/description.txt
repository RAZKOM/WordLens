Say your guesses. Hold the touch bar, say a word, and let go. A card beside the board lists what it heard, best match first, including words that sound alike, like THERE and THEIR. Swipe to the one you meant and tap. You can also spell with letters or with alpha, bravo, charlie. Prefer typing? Swipe the keyboard and tap, or switch hold back to delete in the menu.

A solid tile means the letter is in the word and in the right place. A circle means the letter is in the word, in the wrong place. A strike means the letter is not in the word. The keyboard shows the same marks, so you can see which letters are left. If you hide the keyboard to just ENTER, DEL, and CLEAR, the alphabet underneath does the same job: a filled dot for the right spot, an open dot for the wrong spot, and letters that are out drop away. Your current streak and your best streak sit in the top corner.

A guess has to be five letters and in the word list. If it is short, the glasses tell you that you need five letters. If it is not a word, they say it is not in the list. Those notes stay up long enough to read. Win, and you see how many guesses it took. Miss all six, or give up, and the answer is shown before the next word begins.

Stats keeps your streak, best streak, games played, wins, win rate, and a chart of how many guesses each win took, plus losses. The latest game is the brightest bar. Your streak and that chart stay after you leave and come back.

WordLens uses the glasses microphone only while you hold to talk. Speech is recognized on your phone, offline; nothing you say is recorded, kept, or sent anywhere. WordLens does not use the network, the camera, or your location. What it remembers stays on the device.
