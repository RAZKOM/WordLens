WordLens is an endless five-letter word game for your glasses. Each round gives you six guesses. The moment a round ends, the next word is dealt, as many times as you want to keep playing.

Play from the touch bar. Swipe to move along the letters, and tap to type the one in the centre. Hold to delete the last letter. Tap, then hold, to open the menu. Enter guess and Backspace sit at the top, so submitting or deleting is one tap away. Help, Stats, Give up, and Quit are in that same menu. Double-tap asks you to leave, and the glasses confirm before the game closes.

Marks use shape, so they stay clear on the display. A solid tile means the letter is in the word and in the right place. A circle means the letter is in the word, in the wrong place. A strike means the letter is not in the word. The same marks appear on the keyboard beside your current streak and your best streak.

A guess has to be five letters and in the word list. If it is short, the glasses tell you that you need five letters. If it is not a word, they say it is not in the list. Those notes stay up long enough to read. Win, and you see how many guesses it took. Miss all six, or give up, and the answer is shown before the next word begins.

Help restates the gestures and the three marks. Stats keeps your streak, best streak, games played, wins, win rate, and a chart of how many guesses each win took, plus losses. The latest game is the brightest bar. Your streak and that chart stay after you leave and come back.

WordLens does not use the network, the microphone, the camera, or your location. What it remembers stays on the device.
