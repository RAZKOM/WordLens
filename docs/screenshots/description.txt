WordLens is an endless five-letter word game for your glasses. Each round gives you six guesses. The moment a round ends, the next word is dealt, as many times as you want to keep playing.

Say your guesses. Hold the touch bar, say a word, and let go. A card next to the board lists what it heard, best match first, including words that sound alike, like THERE and THEIR. Swipe to the one you meant and tap, and it goes in as your guess. You can also spell with letters or with alpha, bravo, charlie, and those letters are added to the row instead. While it listens, the card shows a moving row of dots, so you know it is working. Speech is recognized on your phone, offline. Nothing you say is sent anywhere, and the microphone is only on while you hold.

Or type on a full keyboard. Swipe to walk the keys, and brackets follow you instantly. Tap to type a letter, or tap ENTER to guess. If you mostly talk, the menu can swap the keyboard for just ENTER, DEL, and CLEAR, with the alphabet underneath showing which letters are in the right spot, in the wrong spot, or out. The menu can also make holding delete a letter instead of listening. Tap, then hold, to open the menu. Help, Stats, Give up, and Quit are there too. Double-tap asks you to leave, and the glasses confirm before the game closes.

Marks use shape, so they stay clear on the display. A solid tile means the letter is in the word and in the right place. A circle means the letter is in the word, in the wrong place. A strike means the letter is not in the word. The same marks appear on the keyboard, so you can see which letters are left. Your current streak and your best streak sit above the card.

A guess has to be five letters and in the word list. If it is short, the glasses tell you that you need five letters. If it is not a word, they say it is not in the list. Win, and you see how many guesses it took. Miss all six, or give up, and the answer is shown before the next word begins.

Help explains the gestures, talking, and the three marks. Stats keeps your streak, best streak, games played, wins, win rate, and a chart of how many guesses each win took, plus losses. Your streak and that chart stay after you leave and come back.

WordLens uses the glasses microphone only while you hold to talk. It does not use the network, the camera, or your location. What it remembers stays on the device.
