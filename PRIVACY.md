# WordLens privacy policy

WordLens does not collect, store, or share personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or
tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

## Microphone

WordLens uses the G2 glasses microphone only while you hold the touch bar to say a guess. The audio is
processed on your phone by an offline speech recognizer included in the app, kept in memory only until your
guess is recognized, and then discarded. It is never recorded to storage or sent anywhere. You can turn
voice off by switching hold to delete in the menu.

## On your device

WordLens saves your streak, statistics, the round in progress, and two settings (what holding does, and
whether the full keyboard is shown) in the Even app's local storage on your phone. The speech model is
unpacked into that storage the first time voice is used, so it loads faster; it contains no personal data.
All of this stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall WordLens.
